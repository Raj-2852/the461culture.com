Indigenous Tribal Heritage

ASR Agency & Eastern Ghats

Our primary mission is celebrating and documenting the unique lifestyles, folklore, traditions, and music of tribal communities in the Alluri Sitharama Raju district. We explore weekly tribal shandies (haats), ancient customs, and traditional Dhimsa dance steps passed down through generations.

Araku Valley Coffee Trails

Farm to Cup Chronicles

Discover the story behind world-famous organic Arabica coffee grown in high altitudes under shaded silver oak trees by local tribal farmers. We capture the complete journey from red cherry harvesting, pulping, drying, to artisanal roasting with local growers.

Shade-Grown Arabica Tribal Cooperatives Coffee Tasting

Hidden Waterfalls & Treks

Off-The-Beaten Trails

Moving past standard tourist stops to explore untamed, crystal streams tucked deep into the dense ghats. From Katiki and Chaparai to hidden spring waterfalls, our vlogs offer GPS routes, trek difficulty guides, safety protocols, and off-road 4x4 tips.

Cloud Peaks & Winter Frost

Lambasingi & Vanjangi

Experience the 'Kashmir of Andhra Pradesh' with winter sub-zero temperatures, dense morning fog, sunrise cloud camping, and the viral 'Megha Samudram' ocean of clouds at Vanjangi peak. We provide season forecasts and sunrise checkpoint guides.

About Me

Ramesh Killo • Storyteller & Creator

Ramesh Killo

Alluri Sitharama Raju District, Andhra Pradesh, India

I'm Ramesh Killo, a content creator from Alluri Sitarama Raju District, Andhra Pradesh, India. With a background in Computer Science and Engineering and a passion for storytelling, I aim to explore the world beyond the ordinary and bring meaningful, informative, and thought-provoking content to a global audience.

My educational journey began at APTWR School, Allamputtu, followed by my intermediate education at APTWR College, Pedabayalu. I later graduated with a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering from BITS College, Bakkannapalem, Visakhapatnam.

In December 2023, I began my journey into social media content creation. What started as a creative pursuit has grown into a passion for discovering fascinating facts, exploring untold stories, understanding the world around us, and presenting information in an engaging and accessible way.

Through my content, I strive to uncover lesser-known stories, explore diverse perspectives, explain intriguing concepts, and share valuable insights about science, nature, human behaviour, culture, and the world we live in.

Coming from the beautiful landscapes of Alluri Sitarama Raju District, I carry a deep appreciation for nature, local culture, and the stories that often remain unexplored. My vision is to connect local experiences with global knowledge, making information accessible, meaningful, and interesting to people from different backgrounds.

✨ My Vision

My goal is to build a platform that goes beyond ordinary content—a place where curiosity leads to discovery, knowledge inspires understanding, and stories from every corner of the world find their way to a wider audience.

I believe that there is always something new to learn, something fascinating to discover, and a story worth telling.

Welcome to my world of exploration, knowledge, and discovery.
